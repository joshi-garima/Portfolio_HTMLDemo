The Best Movies According to Garima

My top 3 movies of all Time

Tangled

Tangled is a fun and heartwarming Disney movie about Rapunzel, a spirited princess with 70 feet of magical, glowing golden hair. Locked away in a hidden tower by the wicked Mother Gothel, Rapunzel dreams of escaping to see the mysterious floating lanterns that light up the night sky every year on her birthday. Her life changes forever when a charming thief named Flynn Rider stumbles into her tower. The two strike a deal and set off on a thrilling adventure filled with catchy songs, funny sidekicks, and a journey that helps Rapunzel discover who she truly is.

Piku

Piku is a heartwarming 2015 Indian comedy-drama film directed by Shoojit Sircar. The story follows Piku Banerjee, a successful architect, and her aging, grumpy, and hypochondriac father, Bhaskor, who is hilariously obsessed with his bowel movements. When Bhaskor insists on traveling from Delhi to his ancestral home in Kolkata, Piku ends up on a quirky road trip with him and Rana, the stressed-out owner of a local taxi service who decides to drive them himself. The movie beautifully explores the loving yet chaotic relationship between a modern daughter and her demanding father, brought to life through unforgettabl performances by Deepika Padukone, Amitabh Bachchan, and Irrfan Khan.

Queen

Queen is a highly acclaimed 2014 Indian comedy-drama film directed by Vikas Bahl. The story revolves around Rani Mehra, a shy and conservative Punjabi girl from Delhi whose life is turned upside down when her fiancé, Vijay, abruptly calls off their wedding just two days before the ceremony. Devastated but determined, Rani decides to go on their pre-booked European honeymoon entirely by herself. As she travels through Paris and Amsterdam, she steps out of her comfort zone, makes eclectic international friends, and learns to navigate life on her own terms. Powered by a brilliant, career-defining performance by Kangana Ranaut, the movie is a beautiful and empowering celebration of self-discovery, independence, and womanhood.
